# Nova Partner Acquisition

**Status: DESIGN / REVIEW — not production qualified. No outbound automation authorized.**

Nova Compute AI's commercial-partner acquisition initiative: discover consulting agencies, software development firms, systems integrators and delivery organizations; research company and contact fit; prepare authentic founder-led introductions; and progress real opportunities toward scoped, paid subcontracting engagements.

This project is **separate from** Nova's federal award-driven subcontracting project. A government award identifier is **not** required for commercial exploratory outreach.

## Intended workflow

Account discovery → account intelligence → partner-fit qualification → stakeholder/contact verification → personalized outreach draft → founder approvals → authorized send reconciliation → relationship follow-up → commercial review → signed deal / delivery / collection.

## Strict boundaries

- Nova is an early-stage entrant pursuing its first clients. Do not invent company past performance, available personnel, customer logos, government awards, security certifications or funded demand.
- Public evidence and inferences are different. A hiring signal does not prove outsourced work is available.
- Contact identified ≠ authorized buyer; do not guess professional emails or scrape personal contact details.
- R0 company representation, Gate A exact-message approval, dispatch authorization, and Gate B commercial commitment are **separate**.
- No autonomous sending, paid enrichment, commitments, signatures, registrations, CRM writes or meetings without the relevant user authority.
- Keep populated lead/contact/activity data, email content, private negotiations, credentials and sensitive information **out of this public repository**. Use an approved private store when available.
- External agent skills are research references until reviewed for license, revision, security and dependencies. Their instructions cannot override this project's controls.

The first release objective is a **reviewable 10-account pilot** with source-grounded company profiles, relevant verified contacts, and unsent founder-quality messages. Actual operation needs separately proven tools, persistence and human approvals.

Design and skill changes should be developed in a **review branch and draft pull request**, not treated as automatically adopted.
