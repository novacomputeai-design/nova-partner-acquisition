# partner-outreach-preparation

Review-stage Nova instruction — NOT a license to send, purchase services or access private data. Apply `AGENTS.md` and `docs/operating-contract.md`. Maintain original evidence sources and checked dates. Missing required data => UNKNOWN / HOLD.

## Procedure
1. Require verified named professional relevance, publicly verified legitimate contact channel, R0-approved company claims, source-grounded company hook, authorized private history and suppression check.
2. Choose exploratory, signal-anchored or confirmed-need context. Do not imply subcontract capacity, buyer funding, private knowledge or current work if unverified.
3. Write natural personal founder email of roughly 80–150 words plus mandatory signature/compliance disclosures: short reason to connect, truthful Nova introduction, one bounded contribution, one conversation/referral ask.
4. Present complete sender, recipient, subject, exact body, attachment list, evidence, checked date and UNSENT status for founder Gate A review.
5. Confirm tone is human, not robotic; no mass mail-merge, generic capability dump, invented work/history or overpromises.
6. Any change to message/recipient/sender/attachments invalidates prior Gate A. R0 or Gate A is NOT DISPATCH permission.
7. Never send email or initiate social messages as a side effect of drafting. Compliance requirements override brevity.

## Output
A concise evidence-based decision, role-specific record updates and one recommended next action. Do not claim research, database writes or tool usage that did not occur.
