# partner-acquisition-orchestrator

Review-stage Nova instruction — NOT a license to send, purchase services or access private data. Apply `AGENTS.md` and `docs/operating-contract.md`. Maintain original evidence sources and checked dates. Missing required data => UNKNOWN / HOLD.

## Procedure
1. Establish requested workflow and identify minimum specialists; commercial mode never demands federal award PIID.
2. Confirm legal account identity and available authorized private contact history; keep federal and commercial opportunity records separate but reconcile shared person/organization identity.
3. Route discovery → account intelligence → stakeholder contact → fit decision → founder email draft, or directly to conditional commercial review after actual buyer need.
4. Propagate provenance, evidence class, open uncertainties, recommendation and immutable identifiers between stages. No skill can upgrade a hypothesis into buyer-confirmed need.
5. Present founder decision card with account, why now, one possible work package, relevant individual and channel, UNSENT email if ready, gate statuses and next action.
6. Keep R0, Gate A, DISPATCH and Gate B distinct. Only founder can approve; never imply approval from role selection or generic consent. No autonomous sending or contracting.
7. On errors show partial accurate findings and blocked step rather than substitute invented sources or contacts. Runtime skill selection is distinct from merely reading these files.

## Output
A concise evidence-based decision, role-specific record updates and one recommended next action. Do not claim research, database writes or tool usage that did not occur.
