# partner-account-intelligence

Review-stage Nova instruction — NOT a license to send, purchase services or access private data. Apply `AGENTS.md` and `docs/operating-contract.md`. Maintain original evidence sources and checked dates. Missing required data => UNKNOWN / HOLD.

## Procedure
1. Review official firm website, service/practice pages, dated press releases, public case studies, job postings, technology offerings and partnership references.
2. For each claim store original URL, publisher, publication/update date if visible, checked-at and VERIFIED_FACT/PUBLIC_SIGNAL/PARTNERSHIP_HYPOTHESIS/UNKNOWN.
3. Explain what company actually does, likely delivery function, technical stack only when sourced, and whether proposed Nova services complement rather than duplicate its existing offering.
4. Propose one bounded technical service slice, such as integration, automation, AI evaluation, data workflow or architecture assessment. Label unverified scope as hypothesis.
5. Assess possible self-performance, subcontracting constraints, regulation, client-confidentiality and competitive overlap. Unknown buyer intent remains UNKNOWN.
6. Create a short company brief sufficient for fit scoring and role-specific stakeholder mapping. No unsupported claim about budget or private staffing shortage.

## Output
A concise evidence-based decision, role-specific record updates and one recommended next action. Do not claim research, database writes or tool usage that did not occur.
