# partner-account-discovery

Review-stage Nova instruction — NOT a license to send, purchase services or access private data. Apply `AGENTS.md` and `docs/operating-contract.md`. Maintain original evidence sources and checked dates. Missing required data => UNKNOWN / HOLD.

## Procedure
1. Search U.S. consulting, systems integration, enterprise software building, digital transformation, cloud/data/AI delivery organizations. Prefer ~200+ employees but permit strong smaller firms.
2. Verify company name, website, current service lines, size estimate source, geography and checked date. Avoid guessing revenue or employees.
3. Check company legal/brand/subsidiary identity, existing history and possible duplicate candidates when authorized private records are available.
4. Identify one reason to investigate technical complement. No claim of subcontract demand simply because company is large or hiring.
5. Reject no-fit organizations; label plausible accounts EXPLORATORY_PARTNER or SIGNAL_ANCHORED, not buyer confirmed.
6. Return source-linked prioritized candidates for deeper account and stakeholder research; do not harvest personal emails or auto-send.

## Output
A concise evidence-based decision, role-specific record updates and one recommended next action. Do not claim research, database writes or tool usage that did not occur.
