# partner-commercial-review

Review-stage Nova instruction — NOT a license to send, purchase services or access private data. Apply `AGENTS.md` and `docs/operating-contract.md`. Maintain original evidence sources and checked dates. Missing required data => UNKNOWN / HOLD.

## Procedure
1. Require actual buyer-confirmed scope/requirement, not just generic interest. Verify contracting entity, purchasing authority, and any lead/subcontract tier.
2. Inspect SOW/version, milestones, acceptance, dependencies, data access, security, IP, licensing, confidentiality and competing obligations.
3. Verify resourcing, specialists, availability and mobilization in writing; do not claim a salaried engineering bench or guaranteed start date without proof.
4. Build cost and price assumptions separately. Check margin and cash timing, payment conditions, flowdowns, insurance, indemnity, termination and scope change.
5. Label UNKNOWN/ASSUMED items and recommend PROCEED, NEGOTIATE, DEFER or NO_BID with clear business risks.
6. Gate B founder approval required for commercial proposal, signature, pricing and delivery commitments; this skill may not submit or sign.
7. Distinguish EXECUTED_AGREEMENT, DELIVERY, ACCEPTANCE, INVOICED and CASH_COLLECTED with evidence; no automatic contract-win claims.

## Output
A concise evidence-based decision, role-specific record updates and one recommended next action. Do not claim research, database writes or tool usage that did not occur.
