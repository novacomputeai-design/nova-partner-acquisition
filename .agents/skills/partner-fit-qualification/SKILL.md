# partner-fit-qualification

Review-stage Nova instruction — NOT a license to send, purchase services or access private data. Apply `AGENTS.md` and `docs/operating-contract.md`. Maintain original evidence sources and checked dates. Missing required data => UNKNOWN / HOLD.

## Procedure
1. Verify company identity and source-backed tech/service fit before assigning scores.
2. Apply proposed 100-point prioritization: complementarity 25; bounded first project 20; relevance/public signals 20; delivery/accessibility 20; named-contact accessibility 15. Explain each score and unknown.
3. Assign PRIORITIZE at 70+, RESEARCH at 50–69, DEPRIORITIZE below 50, overriding score to HOLD for privacy, suppressions, legal and contact risk.
4. Differentiate EXPLORATORY_PARTNER, SIGNAL_ANCHORED and BUYER_CONFIRMED. Public projects and hiring are signals, not confirmed subcontract orders.
5. Recommend one modest, testable Nova contribution aligned to approved capability; do not promise staffing, dates, pricing or performance.
6. Explain buyer unknowns and propose a single appropriate next question. Scores do not grant permission to contact.

## Output
A concise evidence-based decision, role-specific record updates and one recommended next action. Do not claim research, database writes or tool usage that did not occur.
