# partner-stakeholder-discovery

Review-stage Nova instruction — NOT a license to send, purchase services or access private data. Apply `AGENTS.md` and `docs/operating-contract.md`. Maintain original evidence sources and checked dates. Missing required data => UNKNOWN / HOLD.

## Procedure
1. From company brief determine the buying or delivery function for Nova's proposed support slice; do not assume CTO or alliance manager owns subcontract procurement.
2. Search public official professional biographies and company directories for current named staff. Prefer delivery/practice, then sourcing/resourcing/supplier professionals; CEO/CTO only where duties fit.
3. Verify employer, current title, documented responsibilities, source URL, publication/checked date, and legitimate professional business channel. Note role freshness and any contradiction.
4. Classify DELIVERY_DECISION_CONTACT only if authority supported, PROFESSIONAL_REFERRAL_CONTACT for a credible routing professional, RESEARCH_ONLY if role/channel uncertain, DO_NOT_CONTACT where suppressed.
5. Seek one primary and, when appropriate, one alternate. Do not simultaneously propose contacting multiple people without an independent reason.
6. Consult authorized PRIVATE federal/commercial contact history before recommending a NEW_INTRO; missing access leaves send readiness HOLD.
7. Never guess email patterns, scrape personal addresses or assume that a public profile supplies a direct business email. Generic route only after failed named-person research and explicit founder exception.

## Output
A concise evidence-based decision, role-specific record updates and one recommended next action. Do not claim research, database writes or tool usage that did not occur.
