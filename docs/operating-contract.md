# Nova Commercial Partner Acquisition — Operating Contract v1.0 Candidate

**Status:** PROPOSED / REVIEW — not operationally qualified or production approved (2026-10-29).

## Objective
Acquire Nova Compute AI's first paying clients through small, scoped technical subcontracting or consulting engagements with established consulting agencies, systems integrators, IT service firms and software builders. The federal award-based project remains distinct.

## Account discovery and evidence
Prefer U.S. consulting/software/technology-delivery organizations with approximately 200+ staff (smaller firms can qualify on compelling evidence). Search account websites, service lines, business units, tech practices, public project statements, partner programs, and dated expansion/hiring signals. Distinguish verified facts from public signals, partnership hypotheses, buyer-confirmed needs, and unknowns. A hiring announcement is not evidence of an open subcontract. Keep source URL/publisher/date/checked_at for each material claim.

## Provisional account-fit score
Complementarity 25, plausible small delivery slice 20, publicly evidenced relevance 20, delivery/accessibility 20, relevant named-contact route 15 = 100. 70+ PRIORITIZE, 50–69 RESEARCH, <50 DEPRIORITIZE; thresholds are provisional, not validated win predictions. Hard compliance/contact gates always override score. Classify EXPLORATORY_PARTNER, SIGNAL_ANCHORED, or BUYER_CONFIRMED. Exploratory introductions are allowed without a published subcontract.

## Stakeholders and channels
Research relevant delivery/practice/engineering leaders, subcontract and resource managers, partner/alliances leaders when their responsibility fits, and executive leadership only on role evidence. Record name, verified current employer and duties, source date, legitimate professional business route, authority type (decision versus referral), and alternative where possible. Confirm current role before dispatch, tentatively within 30 days. No scraped personal addresses or inferred email patterns. Named-professional first; generic channels require recorded named research failure and founder exception. Unknown contact or unverified business channel => HOLD.

## Founder-approved value proposition
Nova has no corporate customer history yet. Distinguish founder professional experience, internal demos, corporate technical capabilities, conditional personnel and actual delivery. Never invent staff, client deployments, certifications, pricing, rates or start dates. Propose one modest relevant contribution: a defined integration, automation, data preparation, AI evaluation/prototype or architecture work package. Before agreement, verify staffing and actual terms.

## Human and authentic first outreach
One evidenced reason for contacting this company/person, brief truthful Nova introduction, one bounded contribution, one easy conversation/referral ask. Usually 80–150 natural words plus compliance material. No mass template substitution, private knowledge claims, unsupported client work or assertions that subcontract demand exists. Output complete subject/body/sender/recipient/attachments and evidence in an UNSENT review packet.

## Gates
- R0: founder approves exact scope/version of Nova corporate representations.
- Gate A: founder approves exact sender/recipient/subject/body/attachments; material change invalidates it.
- DISPATCH: separate explicit founder authorization for that exact Gate A packet; no blanket autonomous send.
- Gate B: founder approves a real buyer requirement, delivery, cost, terms and commitments before proposal/submission/signature.
No skill grants itself approval.

## Compliance, memory, and email behavior
Verify actual authorized sender, domain SPF/DKIM/DMARC and relevant B2B commercial email and privacy compliance before pilot; implement valid business sender identity, postal address and opt-out. Review cross-border jurisdictions separately. Suppress opt-outs at their applicable person or company scope.

Maintain PRIVATE records for ACCOUNT, PERSON, OPPORTUNITY, MESSAGE, ACTIVITY, SUPPRESSION, DEAL and approvals. Identity and contact history are shared across federal/commercial segments; opportunities and sector-specific qualification remain separate. Bounced address suppresses that route, not automatically the entire organization; do not evade an opt-out via a colleague. Reconcile parent/subsidiary and person/job changes before dedupe. Public repository must contain only code, instructions, synthetic tests and blank schemas.

DRAFTED → GATE_A_APPROVED → DISPATCH_AUTHORIZED → SEND_ATTEMPTED → SENT_CONFIRMED; exception states HOLD, SEND_FAILED, SEND_UNCERTAIN. Assign operation id and message fingerprint; provider timeout means no retry without inspecting Sent Items and message ID. Only authorized send tool and permitted private ledger writes; read back actual outcomes. SENT does not imply delivery/read/reply.

## Follow-up and real commercial progression
Classify INTERESTED, REFERRAL, REQUEST_INFORMATION, NO_CURRENT_NEED, NOT_INTERESTED, OPT_OUT, BOUNCE, OUT_OF_OFFICE, AMBIGUOUS and NO_REPLY_OBSERVED. Suggested follow-up: founder-reviewed after 5–7 business days; a second 7–10 days later if justified, not automatically. Stop on opt-out, rejection, ongoing conversation or conflicting evidence.

INTRODUCED → ENGAGED → BUYER_DISCOVERY → QUALIFIED_REQUIREMENT → COMMERCIAL_REVIEW → PROPOSAL_ISSUED → EXECUTED_AGREEMENT → DELIVERY → INVOICED → CASH_COLLECTED, with event evidence for each. Verify buyer authority, scope, dependencies, resource availability, customer data/IP/security, price-cost/margin, cash exposure, payment/termination and liability before commercial proposal. Signed value is not cash.

## Pilot and acceptance
Pilot targets are approximately 10 research-qualified companies/week, one primary and optional alternate person per company, up to 10 individually founder-reviewable introductions. No forced sends. Separate measured account research, approval, actual send, bounce, replies, meeting, need, proposal, agreement, invoice, payment. No assumed conversion percentage.

Fail closed on unknown suppression, unverified contact, blocked history, compliance/sender authorization missing, contradictory facts or capacity. Required acceptance: Track A static, Track B native adversarial, Track C approved connector/private persistence, Track D founder-reviewed real pilot. Only founder can authorize production. The current version is a design specification, not proof that any skill is executable.
